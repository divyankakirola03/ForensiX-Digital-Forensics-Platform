About ForensiX

Digital Forensics Investigation Platform

Project Overview

ForensiX is a web-based digital forensic investigation platform designed to assist in the preliminary analysis and management of digital evidence.

The platform provides automated file hashing, metadata extraction, EXIF analysis, duplicate evidence detection, rule-based risk assessment, chain of custody tracking and forensic report generation.

Key Features

  • Digital Evidence Upload
  • MD5 and SHA-256 Hash Generation
  • Metadata Extraction
  • EXIF Analysis
  • Duplicate Evidence Detection
  • Risk Assessment
  • Chain of Custody Logging
  • PDF Forensic Report Generation
  • Case Dashboard
